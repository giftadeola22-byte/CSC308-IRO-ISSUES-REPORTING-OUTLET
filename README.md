## 📌 Project Overview
**IRO (Issues Reporting Outlet)** is a web-based reporting platform designed to collect, manage, and facilitate the resolution of disputes and grievances between **AAUA Students** and **Host Community Indigenes** (Akungba, Iwaro, Etioro, Ayegunle, etc.)[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span). 

The platform serves as a digital bridge to ensure transparent communication, proper categorization of community issues, and rapid response handling by authorities[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span).
